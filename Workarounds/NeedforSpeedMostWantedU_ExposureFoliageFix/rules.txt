This pack measures the brightness in the tone mapping shader instead and scales scene, bloom and lens dirt accordingly. The default was tuned with screenshots from a real Wii U as reference.|Foliage: the deferred lighting shaders ignore the undefined glow and specular data at foliage pixels.|Replaces the "Bloom Fix" pack, keep that one disabled. Changes take effect after restarting the game.|Made by TR2N.
